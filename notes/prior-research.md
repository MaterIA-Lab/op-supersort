# Prior local SuperSort research: initial inventory

This note is an editorial inventory created while preparing the project. The exact model identifier for this preparation must be recorded in the corresponding run. The original research was conducted locally before OpenPrototypes and its authors, models, costs and funding must not be invented.

The prior laboratory contains 71 notes, 93 result directories and approximately 3.21 GB of result files. Its index reports 287,213 measured samples and 328,864 exact output checks including warmups and captured subtree outputs; these are not independent datasets. Diagnostic E063 is counted separately. These figures describe that index, not a new audit of every sample.

The old contract focused on ascending uint32 keys without payload, one CPU thread, with allocation and final copying in the timed region. The later experiments used Ubuntu in WSL on an AMD Ryzen 7 5800X. The initial index also contains Windows experiments. These are not independent CPU architectures.

The previous E054 candidate met its then-current 1.25× speed target in 10 of 22 main cases at 10 million elements, against the best of the references included in that experiment. That older goal and test matrix differ from the current 25% time-reduction goal; this does not prove success under the new goal. Later experiments through E072 and their negatives are summarized in the local continuation note. An older local status note stops at E044 and is stale.
